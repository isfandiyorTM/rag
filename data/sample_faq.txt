SECTION 8: BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE CONCEPTS
--------------------------------------------

Q: What is Business Intelligence (BI)?
A: Business Intelligence (BI) is the set of technologies, processes, and practices that transform raw organisational data into meaningful information to support better business decision-making. BI encompasses data collection, data integration, data warehousing, reporting, dashboards, data visualisation, KPI monitoring, and predictive analytics.

Q: What is the difference between BI and Data Analytics?
A: BI focuses primarily on descriptive analytics — answering "what happened?" and "what is happening now?" using historical and current data. Data Analytics is a broader term that includes diagnostic analytics (why did it happen?), predictive analytics (what will happen?), and prescriptive analytics (what should we do?). Modern BI platforms like TechCorp increasingly incorporate all four types.

Q: What is a KPI?
A: A Key Performance Indicator (KPI) is a measurable value that demonstrates how effectively an organisation is achieving its key business objectives. Examples include Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Net Promoter Score (NPS), Inventory Turnover Rate, and Employee Productivity Index. Good KPIs are SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound.

Q: What is ETL?
A: ETL stands for Extract, Transform, Load — the three-step process of pulling data from source systems, cleaning and transforming it into a consistent format, and loading it into a data warehouse or analytical database. Modern platforms often use ELT (Extract, Load, Transform), where transformation happens inside the data warehouse using its compute power.

Q: What is a data warehouse?
A: A data warehouse is a centralised repository designed for querying and analysis rather than transaction processing. It integrates data from multiple sources, applies a consistent schema, and is optimised for read-heavy analytical workloads. Common data warehouses include Amazon Redshift, Google BigQuery, Snowflake, and Azure Synapse Analytics.
